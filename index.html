    What Bend2 does here

    The original garden’s solver and state transitions are written in Bend2. Compiled Bend logic also checks gameplay, marks and hint transitions. Maintained laws cover effects, minimum routes, hints and puzzle admission.

    Where the browser takes over

    JavaScript schedules generated gardens and runs the fast fixed-effect solver, checked against Bend laws and an independent GF(2) oracle. The browser draws the lanterns and handles sound, gestures and storage. Those parts need separate tests; the whole interface is not formally proved.

    Sound

    Sound begins with your first touch. Hold a lantern to preview its reach without making a move.

    Graphics

    Moving reflections and soft light on the glass.

    Silver moonlight reveals dark glass; nearby lanterns add warm reflections.

    Bright pane contours for easier recognition. Dotted recesses stay dark; interiors still show on or off.

    A travelling spark guides your next press. Reduced motion keeps the quiet glow.

    A short, local graphics test. No uploaded scores or automatic setting changes.
